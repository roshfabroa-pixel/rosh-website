who is this guy

A little bit of both

By day, I'm a barista — pulling espresso shots, dialing in grinders, and keeping the bar moving during the rush. By trade, I'm a Virtual Assistant, and whenever tech acts up, I'm the one troubleshooting it.

I like blending the fast-paced, people-first world of hospitality with the logical puzzles of tech support — one keeps me on my feet, the other keeps me thinking. Turns out steaming milk and debugging a stubborn router take the exact same kind of patience.
