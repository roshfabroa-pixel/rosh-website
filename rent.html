A plus-one for your day. Coffee-grade company, tech-support patience, zero awkward silences guaranteed.

So what is this, exactly?

Exactly what it sounds like, and nothing more: you can book me as company — a plus-one for an event, someone to grab coffee with, a hype-friend for a nerve-wracking errand, or an extra pair of hands when your laptop is fighting you at 11pm. Think "rent a friend," not anything else.

This is a real thing people do — for weddings, first dates that need a buffer, gallery openings, or just someone to talk to over a flat white. I show up, I'm good company, and we keep it public and easy.
